## Personalization and continuity

The strongest durable context is tied to an actionable feature. Active goals and events, a structured block, calendar and completions, current anchors, structured check-ins and RPE, confirmed strength sessions, trips, exception patches, and recent proposal decisions can be read by a future review. For example, a previous accepted exception change can be accompanied by completion and subsequent symptom observations. Association is available without attributing those symptoms to the change. [Snapshot inputs](../../../backend-go/internal/coaching/snapshotreads) · [Snapshot payload](../../../backend-go/internal/coaching/snapshots)

There are three distinct continuity horizons. In the same conversation, the coach can reuse the working plan, evidence and athlete's feedback on trade-offs. Across a fresh conversation, an existing draft has a stable ID and a current revision, and the coach can recover the proposed document and its saved evidence context. Across multiple decisions, the next snapshot projects bounded histories, including recent headlines and next checks, prior block decisions and benchmark history where applicable. Snapshot loading takes up to 20 non-invalid proposals before operation-specific history shaping. [Skill](../../../.codex/skills/going-bananas-coach/SKILL.md) · [Coach API](../../../backend-go/internal/proposalhttp/handler.go) · [Snapshot inputs](../../../backend-go/internal/coaching/snapshotreads) · [Snapshot payload](../../../backend-go/internal/coaching/snapshots)

Unconfirmed preferences, declined alternatives, open conversational questions, and nuance beyond the saved short fields can be lost across chats. Check-in notes are filtered. Structured goals and confirmed constraints have identifiable storage. The input assembly reads the profile for cycle anchoring and clinical restrictions. General-purpose memory capture and retrieval is weakly evidenced in the current skill. The backlog’s IDEA-002 describes an older context endpoint and memory/write flow. The current helper contract lists targeted reads, proposal draft writes and explicit undo. Treat older descriptions as historical until reconciled with implementation. [Backlog](../../backlog.md#idea-002--conversational-coach-with-research-and-lasting-context) · [Helper commands](../../../.codex/skills/going-bananas-coach/references/app-access.md)

The accepted change’s rationale survives in the proposal record. `proposal_detail` exposes the structured proposal why and each change’s reason. The prior-decision projection includes headline, next check, change decision, and safe before/after fields. Ordinary per-change reasons and the typed proposal why are absent from this projection. Dated strength omissions are an exception with an explicit retained reason. An accepted change can carry same-day through day-three symptoms for the date window. Those reports are associations and may be unrelated to that run. The current calendar brief can preserve rationale on the current revision, while historical before/after projections omit the brief. Recovering the original reasoning can therefore require targeted proposal detail. [Saved detail](../../../backend-go/internal/proposalhttp/handler.go) · [History loader](../../../backend-go/internal/coaching/snapshotreads) · [History projection](../../../backend-go/internal/coaching/snapshots)

An acceptance check for stronger continuity would save a distinctive reason and uncertainty, partially accept a draft, and open a fresh rolling or exception review after a recorded outcome. Verify that the accepted action, its reason, relevant unresolved trigger and feedback are recoverable together with bounded context, and that rejected alternatives cannot be mistaken for the accepted action.

Proposal freshness is valuable for continuity. The server ties proposals to a snapshot fingerprint, calendar/block bases, and selected KB evidence. Relevant drift can require a fresh snapshot and revision. Tests exercise source-only and verified-knowledge drift, invalid revisions preserving the old draft, and revision conflicts. The risk is interaction cost if valid drafts often become stale before the athlete can review them. Frequency and recovery time are unmeasured. ## Uncertainty and provenance

- The persisted proposal separates observed facts, research, inference, and athlete priority in its rationale. It has structured uncertainty, practical change triggers, next check, per-session effort and adjustment rules. Brief fields have character limits and the UI resolves source IDs to titles. This makes a pending decision easier to scan and lets later analysis inspect its stated assumptions. [Proposal schema](../../../backend-go/internal/coaching/drafts/parse.go) · [Proposal format](../../../.codex/skills/going-bananas-coach/references/proposal-format.md)
- Useful signs of careful interpretation include using comparable runs, naming missing streams and effort data, avoiding a composite physiology score, and labeling tentative training paces. The skill gives these instructions. Current validation and retrieval tests do not measure whether the coach consistently follows them. [Review workflows](../../../.codex/skills/going-bananas-coach/references/coaching-workflows.md) · [Coaching cases](../../../running_coach_kb/evaluation/coaching_cases.md)
- Compact history limits context and data exposure. Structured drill-down access can recover individual activity execution, long runs, recovery nights, version diffs and metric reasons. Older workout progression is a backlog item, and the helper's query list contains no broad longitudinal coaching outcomes query. A fresh reviewer may need a narrowly sourced question or evidence window to reconstruct an older decision. ## Research reuse, context costs, and gaps

The checked-in design uses the verified local library first, with outside research reserved for concrete gaps, disputed claims, direct requests, or higher-priority verification. Intake records the question, sources, access, limitations and proposed use. Unverified candidates stay outside runtime evidence. A verified promotion updates provenance, evidence records and manifest version. Snapshot freshness then respects the new evidence. Optional small research agents take a bounded question and minimal context. The same head coach verifies decisive primary claims and owns synthesis. [Intake](../../../.codex/skills/going-bananas-coach/references/research-intake.md) · [Model and research](../../../.codex/skills/going-bananas-coach/references/model-and-research.md) · [Library intake](../../../running_coach_kb/research/coaching_web_intake.md)

Known context controls include operation-shaped sections, history caps, optional drill-down, evidence reuse within a review, and shorter persisted fields. The KB estimate is characters divided by four, rounded up. It warns when an operation's nominal token budget is exceeded, including 3,000 for rolling and 9,000 for exception, with a 200,000-character hard ceiling for routed text. Snapshot `approx_tokens` estimates payload plus KB text. It omits accumulated conversation, the model's reasoning and output, tool wrappers, and any delegated research. It is a proxy for input size. No elapsed-time, actual token, or price comparison was run. A routing snapshot followed by a block snapshot, long counterproposal threads, and delegated research are concrete paths that can raise context or latency. The app may need both snapshots for routing, while the skill can read the first selectively. ### Questions about reasoning quality

| Question | Evidence to obtain |
| --- | --- |
| Where does the coach disagree with the athlete or miss a material constraint? | A synthetic corpus of multi-turn trajectories and consented, minimized outcome observations. Compare first recommendation, counterproposal, accepted outcome, and next review against explicit expected behaviors. |
| When should an input change a decision, trigger a question, or leave training alone? | Paired cases that differ in a single fact and then in interacting facts. Include missing data, uncertain calibration, noisy wearables, verified constraints, and symptomatic feedback under recent load. Require the model to state what would change its decision. |
| Which recipes and evidence are missing when building a plan? | Record unresolved decision questions and whether each gap came from absent facts, route selection, missing research, or interpretation. Test using verified excerpts first. Review distance/time goals, fueling where material to long sessions, return-to-running progression, and hybrid-strength trade-offs against the evidence actually routed. |
| Which information survives a new conversation and matters later? | Compare a review resumed from a fresh chat after acceptance with one in the original chat. Look for repeated questions, forgotten commitments, and correct use of the last check and revision. Identify the smallest durable facts that would resolve those failures. |
| What is the usable context and cost envelope? | Collect response sizes, per-review turns, snapshot refreshes, actual usage and time to first useful recommendation with consent and synthetic fixtures. Benchmark single-head coaching against targeted delegation for the same missing-evidence task. Price estimates require actual usage and applicable billing terms. |

## Operating without a premium coaching dependency

A deterministic experiment with the Python reference loader measured research text alone. With no athlete data, the minimum rolling route was **5,572 proxy tokens** against a 3,000 warning budget. The minimum block route was **8,107** against 5,000, and exception was **7,291** against 9,000. Holding the selection fixed to easy and long recipes plus the recovery and PTT topics raised the routes to 12,839, 15,374 and 14,558 respectively. The experiment called `load_route` with each operation, first with empty selections and then with REC001, REC002 and those two topic paths. Conditional topics load whole files and replace overlapping excerpts. Baseline routes that already exceed budget make a warning alone a poor cost control. Test shorter excerpts for the specific decision while retaining eligibility limits and evidence IDs. These are computed sizes, with no model call. [Python reference loader](../../../backend/app/services/knowledge.py)

An illustrative four-Sunday cycle with four rolling snapshots (including one used for routing) and one additional block snapshot would contain around **154,000 proxy tokens** if each package had its historical median size of 27,000 or 46,000. Actual context use will differ. A routing tool can read selectively, a conversation can reuse evidence, and billing can include replayed input, cached input, output, reasoning, follow-ups and searches. A stored size does not tell us what was transmitted or charged. [Measured denominators](data.md#what-the-saved-reviews-demonstrate)

Measure the actual end-to-end cost of one ordinary review, a block review and a revised draft under the user's available plan. For metered access, multiply uncached input, cached input and output usage by the applicable rates and add research/helper costs. For a subscription, distinguish any **additional** monthly fee required for coaching from access the athlete already has. Use the agreed monthly ceiling, latency and quality as the selection criteria. The current pinned coach policy means affordable independent operation of *personalized proposal generation* remains unproven.

The economical steady state is zero calls for ingestion, matching, checks, daily plan display and the factual weekly recap. Bring one summarized packet with the previous decision, consequential changes, recent coverage and required evidence to an athlete-initiated review and allow bounded drill-down for a disputed fact. Use the same discussion for revisions. Trial a cheaper approved model on synthetic ordinary weeks before routing any real review to it. Assess harder cases separately. If model access lapses, keep the accepted training, local edits, workout outcomes and due-review evidence usable. Extending the accepted horizon will need a new athlete or coach decision.

## Research decisions worth paying for

The KB includes a guide, task workflows, policy candidates, session recipes, topic sections, evidence matrices and a registry that states population, design, access level and limits. For example, the registry documents travel sources S073–S106 verified November 22 even though its headline searched-through field still says November 11. Use record-level dates and source access when judging currency. An abstract check, a hash match and full-text verification answer different questions. An unvalidated topical signal cannot be fixed by buying more general research. | Narrow question | Use before a new search | Outcome that would justify a KB change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which functional observations around an already prescribed PTT routine are sufficiently interpretable to discuss with the clinician at reassessment? | Existing PTT topic, actual routine/clinical instruction, evidence population and co-interventions. | A relevant, supported measure with timing and limits. Otherwise retain a user/clinician-defined benchmark and unknown return criteria. |
| Does a change in pace/HR across these easy runs remain interpretable when terrain, sensor, temperature or effort differs? | Existing metric exclusions, individual protocol and available comparators. | Evidence for a specific eligibility or exclusion rule that alters a repeat/progress decision. If inputs are absent, improve coverage or withhold comparison. |
| For this runner's observed schedule, when does lower-body work warrant moving a key run? | Confirm the lift actually occurred and its proximity, perceived fatigue and prior run response. Use the existing hybrid topic. | A recurring decision that the existing guidance cannot resolve and studies relevant to concurrent recreational training. Keep the uncertainty and individual preference visible. |
| How should a lower-priority race affect taper and return in a low-frequency plan? | Existing race-purpose rules and prior comparable race response. | A genuine unresolved tradeoff with evidence from relevant distances and training levels. An age-group marathon result alone cannot set a secondary 5K taper. |

Record the unresolved decision and what answer would change it. A missing athlete report calls for a question, a misrouted existing excerpt calls for a routing fix, and absent applicable research calls for explicit uncertainty.
