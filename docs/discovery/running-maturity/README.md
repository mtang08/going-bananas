## Evidence and limits

The team traced application code, database models, selected migrations, ingestion and read services, UI, helper, research routes, tests and recorded release checks. Focused checks passed for 313 backend tests, 19 helper tests and 212 frontend tests. Separate synthetic checks exercised recovery-topic selection, measured the KB loader output and tested restriction validation. We reviewed checked-in Playwright journeys and PostgreSQL integration cases. A fresh database integration run was unavailable because the sandbox denied PostgreSQL shared memory for a dedicated cluster and the available test credentials could not authenticate to the existing isolated server. There was no demo listener for a fresh browser journey. Prior release results are dated and identified as such. [Audit](current-state.md#verification) · [Synthetic findings](coaching.md#what-the-coach-actually-sees-during-adaptation) · [Historical acceptance](../../release-checklist.md)

The live inspection used health metadata and read-only aggregate queries over saved decisions and their outcomes. Counts describe the stored sample, including different snapshot generations. No provider sync, training proposal, athlete check-in, calendar edit or model coaching session was initiated. Competitor capabilities are documented by their makers. Roadmap rankings, effort bands and maintenance gates are judgments for this particular product. Training outcomes, usability over months, and actual inference charges remain unmeasured.

## Executive summary

**Maturity.** Going Bananas has a working running analytics product and early evidence of personalized coaching in use. It is approaching **Level 1** as an overall experience and already implements parts of Levels 2 and 3, particularly race-linked blocks, interval assessment, robust recovery baselines and exception patches. Three accepted proposals created calendar versions. Across two transitions to a later accepted review, the saved context included all three eligible earlier runs with completion and RPE. No app check-ins or benchmark outcomes were present. Three runs cannot establish training effectiveness. [Observed use](data.md#what-the-saved-reviews-demonstrate)

**Most consequential gaps.** The athlete's response to a changed dose is often unknowable from execution alone. The optional pain question loses the difference between “no” and unanswered. The coach can see restriction text but proposal validation handles only selected changes in a short window. Some adverse recovery data reaches the snapshot without triggering its intended supplemental topic. A subsequent review can retrieve the accepted sessions more easily than it can recover exactly why they changed and what should be checked next. [Response capture](data.md#data-worth-asking-for) · [Constraint and retrieval findings](coaching.md#what-the-coach-actually-sees-during-adaptation) · [Continuity](intelligence.md#personalization-and-continuity)

**Highest-value discovery.** Ask “what was this change intended to test, and what happened?” once per material decision. Link the accepted dose, comparable previous exposure, recorded effort, delayed response and next decision. This gives the runner a useful answer even when the answer is “we saw the shorter run completed, but still do not know how it felt the following day.” It also gives the owner a way to test future coaches against the same decision. A second opportunity is to make the cost of a Sunday review visible and reduce the evidence packet before changing models. Historical median packet sizes were about 27,000 proxy tokens for rolling and 46,000 for block reviews. [Feature inventory](opportunities.md) · [Cost assessment](intelligence.md#operating-without-a-premium-coaching-dependency)

**Build next.** Close one workout's response loop from Home through the following review, including explicit absence, missing data and safe retry. Address active restrictions, recovery routing and stale decision evidence alongside that foundation. The next weekly coaching feature is follow-through on a material decision. Independent PTT rehab scheduling is the first expansion of the calendar, and can move ahead of optional planning refinements as soon as the feedback and restriction semantics are ready. Respect the 15-minute daily budget and the existing priority ahead of strength/HYROX. [Roadmap](roadmap.md) · [Original rehab request](../../backlog.md#idea-010--integrate-ptt-and-hip-mobility-work-with-benchmarks)

**Deliberate omissions.** Leave lifting execution in Strong. Keep watch publishing, a general scheduling engine, comprehensive nutrition logging, medical diagnosis, a single readiness score, an always-on agent and broad strength/HYROX programming outside this running roadmap. Competing products illustrate valuable patterns in post-workout context and temporary replanning. The local advantage is a reviewed plan that can remember the owner's constraints and learn from the results of specific changes. [Market research](products.md) · [Deferred choices](decisions.md#candidates-to-leave-outside-the-roadmap)

**Architecture.** The existing local database, deterministic services, verified KB, external reasoning step and human-controlled calendar are appropriate. Improve the evidence projection, align its identity with the metric configuration, and preserve stable session IDs and revisions. A future consumer could request dated accepted sessions, time requirements, scheduling constraints and completion state without gaining access to raw health data. A new deployment or cross-domain schema has no present justification. [Boundaries](architecture.md)

**Completion.** Use four ordinary Sunday reviews and one block transition as a proposed acceptance trial. Stop when the athlete can find and execute the accepted plan, tell what happened and why the next decision follows, keep rehab and lifting coordinated, trust the missing-data and restriction behavior, and operate within a personally acceptable time/cost budget. Confirm a restore in isolation and exercise a disrupted week synthetically if necessary. If no recurring decision is still failing, move to maintenance even if several optional features remain on the backlog. [Stop criteria](roadmap.md#a-reasonable-point-to-stop)
