Architecture guide

How Going Bananas works

Your Mac keeps the training record and the accepted calendar. You record what happened during the week, discuss the evidence with Coach in the dashboard or a separate Codex session, and decide which proposed changes to apply.

From a recorded run to your week

Open Home after a run. The “Planned vs done” view brings together the workout you accepted, the recording that arrived and your own account of how it went.

Garmin → recovery trendsSleep, heart-rate variability and resting heart rate can put a night in the context of your recent baseline. Missing readings stay unknown in the review.

Strong → lifting historyYou export and import a CSV. The app can match recorded lifts to planned strength sessions and bring that history into the next review.

Google Calendar → travel contextA read-only sync detects possible trips from flight events. You can confirm, edit or dismiss a trip. The coach sees trip status and availability when planning.

How a Sunday review changes the plan

Sending a message in the dashboard’s Coach page starts Codex on demand through the local API. You can also use the coaching skill in a separate Codex session. The coach reviews the training block if it is missing or due for review. Otherwise, you work through the upcoming sessions together.

When an iPhone check-in reaches the Mac

The optional Home Screen app records morning first-step ankle pain, including on rest days. After the phone is set up for offline use, it can save an observation while the Mac is asleep.
