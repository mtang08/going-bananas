# Head coach and bounded research

Astra Medium (`gpt-6-astra`, reasoning effort `medium`) owns personalized training decisions, final synthesis, and submission. Explicit model/effort selection applies only to a newly spawned agent; it does not change this session. Use a fresh child (`fork_turns="none"`) when changing model and include the skill path and its head-coach role in the minimal task packet. If unavailable, explain the model selection needed; do not misrepresent the current model or block independent read-only work.

Reuse snapshot KB excerpts first. Delegate research only for a genuine missing or disputed claim after checking relevant existing evidence. One simple lookup rarely needs an agent. When delegation is useful, use at most one or two isolated, narrowly scoped helpers: `gpt-6-luna` with `medium` effort for bounded retrieval/extraction, or `gpt-6.1-sol` with `low` effort when extraction is more complex. Give each a neutral research question, relevant existing citations, source criteria, and a brief output limit. Do not send the full conversation, athlete health/training details, credentials, or raw private records to helpers or web queries.

Ask for a compact, source-linked brief containing retrieval/publication dates, population, study design, findings, limitations, relevance, uncertainty, and contradictions. Distinguish accessible primary material from abstracts/snippets. Helpers may fetch and extract; they must not prescribe, submit, accept, or promote claims into the KB. The head coach verifies decisive claims in their primary sources and reconciles conflicting findings before using them. Follow [research-intake.md](research-intake.md) for recording and verified promotion; delegation does not bypass that process.

Small isolated briefs can reduce head-coach context and cost; they do not guarantee fewer total tokens. Subagents typically use more tokens than a comparable single-agent run. Avoid delegation overhead when the head coach can resolve the question directly. Runtime capability and available models remain authoritative. See the official [subagents guide](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-configuration/subagents) for explicit model/effort configuration and token tradeoffs.
