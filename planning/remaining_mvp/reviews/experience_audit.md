## Required guard preservation

Current `agent_plans.submit` serializes writes with a transaction advisory lock, detects changed payload reuse of an idempotency key, checks evidence hash and expected revision, rejects historical activation, and records immutable input/evidence snapshots. Its evidence hash captures observations, confirmed completions, manual plans, KB text and another workflow's plan epoch while avoiding self-triggering review loops. Rollback protects historical, completed and locked sessions and only undoes the latest changed revision; no-change reviews do not hide that revision.

The proposed V2/V3 check (24-hour age plus calendar/block head) does **not** replace source freshness. A new symptom, profile update, activity, recovery observation, anchor or KB change can invalidate advice without moving calendar head. Add a deterministic decision-input fingerprint/source-version vector, recorded with the snapshot and checked at submit and decision time; recompute safety facts at acceptance. Pin the KB digest and selected source records, not merely a package label. Retain explicit conflict errors and no automatic stale resubmission.

For manual decisions, specify atomic decision replay: identical retry returns the original result/version; different selection after a terminal decision conflicts; reject-all and accepted no-change write no calendar version. Calendar, block and anchor selections must be validated as one resulting state, with layer/eligibility rules rechecked after subset selection. Use one transaction/lock for validation and all resulting writes. Rollback creates lineage without rewriting completed facts; UI must describe what was preserved rather than promise byte-for-byte historical restoration.
