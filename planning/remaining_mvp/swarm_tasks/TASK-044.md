## Objective and observable end state
With no calendar (`head === 0`), Settings shows a setup wizard; submitting it saves the profile, saves the strength routine, and bootstraps the calendar (D10), then reads "Calendar created (version 1). Open Home to see today." With a calendar, it shows a setup summary. In both states it lets the user manage goals, events, and intensity anchors and pick the weekly review day; after setup it also edits the strength routine.
