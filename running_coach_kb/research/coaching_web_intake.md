# Coaching web research intake

This log captures outside research used or considered during coaching. It is not
an evidence registry and does not activate coaching rules. Candidate entries are
excluded from runtime routes until verified and promoted. Athlete-specific facts
belong in private app state, not this log.

## Intake and promotion

When a specific gap requires research, append the question, direct source
URL/DOI/PMID, retrieval date, access actually obtained, findings, limitations,
duplicate check, and proposed coaching consequence. Do not create source/evidence
IDs for unverified candidates. Verify the primary material before updating the
source registry, evidence record and relevant topic together; retain IDs and
historical provenance. Update manifest hashes and package version, run integrity
and retrieval tests, then obtain fresh coaching snapshots. A failed verified-KB
load must be repaired rather than bypassed with live search.
