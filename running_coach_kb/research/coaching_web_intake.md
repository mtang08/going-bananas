# Coaching web research intake

This log captures outside research used or considered during coaching. It is not
an evidence registry and does not activate coaching rules. Candidate entries are
excluded from runtime routes until verified and promoted. Athlete-specific facts
belong in private app state, not this log.

## 2026-11-22 — air travel, time-zone change, and training

A targeted outside review asked how air travel, with or without a time-zone change, should change training for a recreational runner who strength trains. An independent check on 2026-11-22 confirmed each promoted citation against its PubMed record or the official page and found all 13 spot-checked numbers in the source text. Full text was read for S080, S096, and S100; the other promoted findings rest on abstracts. No promoted source duplicates an existing DOI or PMID. Promoted together: S073–S106, E-TRAV-01–34 (`references/evidence_matrix.md` › Travel and time-zone addendum), candidate policies P029–P041, `topics/travel_and_time_zones.md`, and cases C35–C39.

| Question/source | Retrieval and disposition | Coaching effect |
|---|---|---|
| Travel fatigue versus jet lag; management consensus and intervention review: S073, S074 | Verified; promoted. | Separate travel load from circadian load; intervention evidence is low certainty. |

## Intake and promotion

When a specific gap requires research, append the question, direct source
URL/DOI/PMID, retrieval date, access actually obtained, findings, limitations,
duplicate check, and proposed coaching consequence. Do not create source/evidence
IDs for unverified candidates. Verify the primary material before updating the
source registry, evidence record and relevant topic together; retain IDs and
historical provenance. Update manifest hashes and package version, run integrity
and retrieval tests, then obtain fresh coaching snapshots. A failed verified-KB
load must be repaired rather than bypassed with live search.
